# About SadQah

## What does "SadQah" mean?

**SadQah** comes from the Arabic word **صدقة (Ṣadaqah)**, often spelled *Sadaqah*. It grows from the Arabic root **ص-د-ق (ṣ-d-q)**, which carries the meanings of *truth*, *sincerity* and *honesty*. The idea is simple and old: when you give, your generosity is proof that your values are real.

In Arabic and Islamic tradition, Sadaqah is **voluntary giving** from the heart. It is different from *Zakat*, which is a fixed, obligatory annual contribution. Sadaqah has no minimum, no deadline and no rulebook. A coin, a meal, a loan that is forgiven, an hour of your time, or a kind word all count. What matters is the sincerity behind it.

You do not need to be Muslim, or religious at all, to feel what this word describes. Almost every culture has its own version of it: *tzedakah*, *dāna*, *daan*, *ubuntu*, "paying it forward". SadQah borrows the Arabic name because it captures something universal: **giving that is honest, quiet and personal.**

## Why give through SadQah?

Most people want to help, and most people hesitate for the same reasons: *Will it really reach someone? Who is it helping? Does my small amount even matter?*

SadQah is built to answer those questions.

- **Your gift reaches a verified person.** Every recipient completes identity, address and income verification before receiving ongoing support. Verification is reviewed so that help goes to people who genuinely need it.
- **Small gifts matter here.** Recipients live on less than $50 a month. A few dollars can buy stock for a street stall, a sewing machine repair, seeds, or a month of school fees. SadQah directs gifts toward small, practical "exit poverty" business capital, not just one-off relief.
- **You can see where your gift went.** Give publicly and see the story you helped write. Give anonymously and use your private Donation Token to follow the same journey without ever revealing who you are.
- **You can hear back.** Recipients can say thank you, to you directly if you gave publicly, or to the whole community if you chose to stay anonymous.
- **Every payment is accounted for.** Each donation is tied to a unique ledger record from the moment you give it until the moment it is paid out. No loose, unexplained transactions.
- **You choose how visible to be.** Public donors earn recognition and leaderboard points. Anonymous donors are completely masked: no name, no email, nothing linked in any public view.

## The benefits of giving (for everyone)

People who give often report that giving does as much for them as it does for the receiver. Here is what SadQah tries to make easy and rewarding:

1. **Meaningful impact for a small amount.** When you give to someone living on a very small monthly income, money goes a long way.
2. **A sense of connection.** Seeing a real person's progress, their region, their small business, their thank-you note, turns a transaction into a relationship.
3. **Dignity for the recipient.** Recipients are supported as entrepreneurs-in-the-making, not as faces of pity.
4. **Peace of mind.** Verified recipients, transparent tracking and a clear ledger mean you know your gift is accounted for.
5. **A habit that builds you up.** Giving regularly, even modestly, is a simple way to act on your values and to make generosity part of how you live.
6. **Community.** Join a global group of people who decided that where someone was born should not decide whether they get a chance.

## How much should I give?

There is no "correct" amount. Sadaqah is voluntary, so **you decide**. If you would like a starting point, many people choose to give a small, steady share of their yearly income:

| Share of yearly income | If you earn $30,000 / year | If you earn $60,000 / year | If you earn $100,000 / year |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1%** (a gentle start) | $300 per year ($25 / month) | $600 per year ($50 / month) | $1,000 per year (about $83 / month) |
| **2.5%** (a balanced habit) | $750 per year ($62.50 / month) | $1,500 per year ($125 / month) | $2,500 per year (about $208 / month) |
| **5%** (a committed giver) | $1,500 per year ($125 / month) | $3,000 per year ($250 / month) | $5,000 per year (about $417 / month) |

**You can give as little as 1% to 5% of your total yearly income, and you can pick any amount you like:**

- A one-time gift of any size, from $1 upwards.
- A monthly gift that spreads your yearly share into easy, painless amounts.
- A custom amount that you set yourself, and change whenever you want.

The SadQah **Giving Calculator** lets you enter your yearly income, move a slider between 1% and 5% (or type your own amount), and see exactly what that means per month and per year, and how many verified recipients your gift could help.

> **A note on honesty.** Sadaqah is voluntary and is not the same thing as Zakat. The 1-5% guide on SadQah is a *suggestion to help you plan generous giving*. It is not a religious ruling, and it does not replace Zakat for people who pay it. If you pay Zakat and want guidance on how it applies to you, please speak to a qualified scholar.
